The Power Rule Machine

The Power Rule is the first "shortcut" you learn in calculus. It tells you how to find the derivative of any variable raised to a power.

If f(x) = axn, then f'(x) = (a × n)xn-1

What just happened?

  • Step 1: You took the original exponent (n) and multiplied it by the coefficient in front.
  • Step 2: You subtracted 1 from the original exponent.
  • The Result: That new expression represents the slope of the original function at any point.
