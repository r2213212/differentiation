\[ f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h} \]

Step-by-Step Expansion for \( x^3 \)

1. Expand \( f(x+h) \) \( (x+h)^3 = x^3 + 3x^2h + 3xh^2 + h^3 \)
2. Subtract \( f(x) \) \( 3x^2h + 3xh^2 + h^3 \)
3. Divide by \( h \) \( 3x^2 + 3xh + h^2 \)
4. Final \( f'(x) \) As \( h \to 0 \), slope is \( \mathbf{3x^2} \).
(At \(x=1\), slope approaches 3)
